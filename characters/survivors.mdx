Survivors are the evasion and objective role in Shattered Reality. Standard survivors have 100 HP and tuned sprint stats that give them an edge in open-field escape, but killers have a raw speed advantage over sustained chases. Survivor ability kits lean toward utility — repositioning, stealth, healing, and area denial — rather than direct combat. Some survivors expose a `Stances` table that swaps `SprintConfig` and ability availability based on an active mode.

<Note>
  All values on this page come directly from each survivor's `Config` ModuleScript under `ReplicatedStorage/Systems/Characters/Survivors/`. Base-config values are shown; skin overrides may change `Info`, `Instances`, and other cosmetic fields.
</Note>

## Kane

Kane ("The Fierce Combatant") is a **Sentinel**-class survivor with the most complex kit on the survivor side. He has two stances — **Agile** and **Aggressive** — each with a distinct `SprintConfig` and a different subset of active abilities. The `SwitchStyle` ability (Ability1) toggles between them.

<Accordion title="Default SprintConfig">

  Applied on spawn before any stance is activated.

  | Field | Value |
  |---|---|
  | `Stamina` / `MaxStamina` | 100 |
  | `WalkSpeed` | 11.5 |
  | `SprintSpeed` | 24 |
  | `SprintLoss` | 12 |
  | `SprintGain` | 24 |

</Accordion>

<Accordion title="Stance: Agile">

  Faster sprint speed and walk speed with a smaller stamina pool. Unlocks the `Crouch` (Sneak) and `Knife` (Deep Cut) abilities.

  | Field | Value |
  |---|---|
  | `MaxStamina` | 90 |
  | `WalkSpeed` | 12.5 |
  | `SprintSpeed` | 26 |
  | `SprintLoss` | 14 |
  | `SprintGain` | 20 |

</Accordion>

<Accordion title="Stance: Aggressive">

  Slower but with a larger stamina pool. Unlocks the `AxeSwing` (Bonebreaker) and `Pistol` (Deadshot) abilities.

  | Field | Value |
  |---|---|
  | `MaxStamina` | 115 |
  | `WalkSpeed` | 10 |
  | `SprintSpeed` | 23 |
  | `SprintLoss` | 11 |
  | `SprintGain` | 25 |

</Accordion>

## Brickbattler

Brickbattler ("The Swift Rocketeer") is a **Sentinel**-class survivor specialising in explosive area denial. His Manager (`Behavior.luau`) handles weapon-visibility tweening for a Sword, FakeSword, and Launcher model, swapping them in/out as abilities activate.

<Accordion title="Sprint & movement">

  | Field | Value |
  |---|---|
  | `Stamina` / `MaxStamina` | 100 |
  | `WalkSpeed` | 11.5 |
  | `SprintSpeed` | 25 |
  | `SprintLoss` | 12 |
  | `SprintGain` | 24.5 |

</Accordion>

<Accordion title="Abilities">

  **Piercing Dash** (`Ability1`)

  | Field | Value |

## JaneDoe

JaneDoe ("The Blissful Widow") is a **Survivalist**-class survivor with slightly reduced health (95 HP) but the fastest default sprint speed of the standard roster at 29. Her `AbilityInfo` is currently empty — she has no active abilities. She is listed in JohnDoe's `Relations` array, which triggers an alternate chase theme during Last Man Standing when she is the final survivor.

<Accordion title="Sprint & movement">

  | Field | Value |
  |---|---|
  | `MaxHealth` | 95 |
  | `Stamina` / `MaxStamina` | 100 |
  | `WalkSpeed` | 11 |
  | `SprintSpeed` | 29 |
  | `SprintLoss` | 14 |
  | `SprintGain` | 25.5 |

</Accordion>

<Accordion title="Abilities">

  JaneDoe has no active abilities (`AbilityInfo = {}`). Her kit relies entirely on movement speed to create distance from the killer.

  <Note>
    JaneDoe's connection to JohnDoe is encoded in his Config under `Relations = { "JaneDoe" }` and `LastManStandingEmotionalData`. Her own Config does not reference this relationship — it is a one-way declaration from the killer side.
  </Note>

</Accordion>

## !Vintage

!Vintage is the limited/expanded variant of the Vintage character, stored in `!Vintage/` and accessed through the `!`-prefix resolution system. Unlike the Nova base config, !Vintage has a dramatically different stat profile — 500 HP, significantly larger stamina pool — and adds two additional abilities: `VoidThrow` and `JoblessOverload`.

<Warning>
  !Vintage's `MaxHealth` of 500 places it far outside normal survivor balance. Game mode logic that assumes survivor HP ≤ 100 will need to account for this value.
</Warning>

## Reading survivor configs at runtime

```luau
local Characters = require(game.ReplicatedStorage.Modules.Data.Characters)

-- Normalized base config
local kane = Characters:GetSurvivorConfig("Kane")
print(kane.MaxHealth)                     --> 100
print(kane.SprintConfig.SprintSpeed)      --> 24
print(kane.Stances.Agile.SprintConfig.SprintSpeed)  --> 26

-- Full merged config with skin applied
local config = Characters.GetActorConfig("Survivor", "Kane", "Dued1Kane")
print(config.SkinName)  --> "Dued1Kane"

-- Single ability config (normalized)
local pistol = Characters:GetAbilityConfig("Survivor", "Kane", "Pistol")
print(pistol.ShootDamage)   --> 40
print(pistol.MaxCharges)    --> 15
```

<Tip>
  Survivors whose folder name begins with `!` (e.g. `!Vintage`) are keyed in `Characters.Survivors` under their full name including the `!`. Pass `"!Vintage"` to `GetSurvivorConfig` to retrieve the config directly. The `!`-stripping behavior of `normalize_search_text` applies to folder discovery (e.g. `GetCharacterFolder`, `find_child_insensitive`) but not to the direct table lookup in `GetSurvivorConfig`.
</Tip>
